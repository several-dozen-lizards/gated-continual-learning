# Four-fact replay and stability under later learning

Freeze before outcomes. Resume each of the three repaired Qwen3.5-2B adapters from `repair/` (seeds 17,29,43), which scored 100% on the previous twelve-fact test. This is a conditional continuation of those same worlds and checkpoints, not an independent replication or a tuned optimum.

Two subsequent waves each introduce four new sites with one beacon-color fact per site. Within each wave, the four colors are balanced and shuffled reproducibly. New names are disjoint from previous subjects. These are supplied accepted facts; this phase isolates replay and interference, not evidence verification. No target or old answer is deliberately contradicted. There are twelve initial facts, sixteen after wave one, and twenty after wave two.

Three arms start from exactly the same repaired adapter: no replay; a uniform four-fact buffer drawn without replacement from the accepted records available before each wave; and full replay of those records. Buffer selection uses subject names and a fixed RNG seed, not evaluation answers or performance. No mandatory protection of the repaired target. Buffer refresh occurs when a wave arrives; it can include prior-wave facts at wave two. The complete external ledger is available to selection, so four-fact training replay is not a claim of a four-fact total storage limit. The same buffers are frozen before outcomes.

Each wave trains its four new facts plus the selected buffer, with fresh AdamW optimizer, LR 0.0002, batch size 4, unchanged rank-16 all-linear LoRA and NF4 setup. Use ten replays per wave as a fixed exploratory compute budget, chosen before results; no tuning or stopping based on evaluation. This differs from the prior twenty-replay repair schedule. A new wave continues from the preceding wave's weights within its arm. Arm execution order is randomized by seed. Every arm receives each new fact equally often, but total training budgets intentionally differ: this tests the replay/quality tradeoff, not equal-compute superiority of a selector. Unseen names alone do not guarantee semantic generalization.

Evaluate baseline and both wave endpoints with the two existing held-out question phrasings. At baseline and the final endpoint also evaluate the four additional phrasings used previously, separately reported; these are already known evaluation templates, not a newly held-out test set. No retrieval in model evaluation. Report repaired-target accuracy and old-error recurrence, other original-fact retention, first-wave retention, second-wave learning, and overall accuracy. Include a frozen/no-training reference on the expanded final world to distinguish preexisting guesses from learning. Such a frozen reference shares the initial weights and is evaluated once per seed.

Primary interpretation: whether the four-fact buffer preserves the repair AND original knowledge while acquiring later facts. Do not call unchanged old performance success when new-fact learning fails. Show per-seed variation and actual buffer membership. Compare example, token and optimizer-step costs, recording wall/CPU/CUDA intervals separately; no energy or FLOP claim. There is no predeclared equivalence margin or inferential power for a claim that the small buffer is equivalent to full replay.

Verify original checkpoint reload equivalence, exact reset between arms, finite losses/probabilities, frozen sources/fixtures/prior receipt hashes, and final small-buffer checkpoint reload on both prompt sets. Preserve all wave checkpoints. These two short waves test immediate interference, not long-term retention, autonomous truth discovery, broad conversational generalization or resident readiness.
